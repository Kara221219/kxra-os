# KXRA subscription pricing research

Date: 1 October 2026. Scope: initial UK launch price for KXRA OS packaged tools. Custom projects remain separately scoped and priced.

## Evidence

- `EXTERNAL RESEARCH` — Canva UK lists Canva Pro at £100 per year and Canva Business at £180 per person per year. Source: https://www.canva.com/en_gb/pricing/
- `EXTERNAL RESEARCH` — Adobe Express positions individual and team creative plans at low monthly per-seat prices. Source: https://www.adobe.com/uk/express/pricing
- `EXTERNAL RESEARCH` — Buffer starts its paid publishing product at $5 per channel per month when billed annually. Source: https://buffer.com/pricing
- `EXTERNAL RESEARCH` — Gamma lists Plus at $9 per seat per month, Pro at $18 and Ultra at $90. Source: https://gamma.app/pricing
- `EXTERNAL RESEARCH` — Zapier lists Professional from $19.99 per month and Team from $69 per month. Source: https://zapier.com/pricing
- `EXTERNAL RESEARCH` — Copy.ai lists self-service access at $29 per month or $24 per month on annual billing. Source: https://www.copy.ai/prices
- `EXTERNAL RESEARCH` — Jasper lists Pro at $69 per month or $59 per month when billed annually. Source: https://www.jasper.ai/pricing
- `FACT` — KXRA OS currently has database-enforced plans, entitlements and usage reservations, but production Stripe, external AI providers and unrestricted public onboarding remain disabled.
- `FACT` — Custom projects use a separate proposal and payment-gate model and are not included in a subscription.

## Interpretation

- `AI INFERENCE` — Single-purpose creative and publishing products cluster below KXRA's proposed price, while broader automation and specialist AI marketing products commonly sit around or above it.
- `HYPOTHESIS` — A small-business buyer will accept a modest premium over a single-purpose tool when KXRA combines governed brand work, project context, approved knowledge and separately scoped delivery in one workspace.
- `ESTIMATE` — £29 per month is low enough for founder-led discovery and high enough to avoid positioning KXRA as a disposable utility, provided expensive image, video and deep-research usage is excluded or separately metered.

## Decision

- `DECISION` — Launch-candidate price: **£29 per organisation per month**, exclusive of VAT or other tax where applicable.
- `DECISION` — Annual option: **£290 per organisation per year**, equivalent to two months free.
- `DECISION` — Custom projects, integrations, high-cost research and generated media are outside the base subscription and require a separate price or credit allowance.
- `DECISION` — Free partner access remains an owner-issued, auditable grant and does not create a paid entitlement.
- `DECISION` — The website may state the reviewed launch price while the offer remains private preview. It must not claim checkout or public availability until the deterministic release manifest passes.

The launch price is a commercial decision, not proof of demand. KXRA should review conversion, activation, retained usage, gross margin and support load after the first 10 paying organisations and before increasing acquisition spend.
