# ADR 0053 — Focused business improvement services use existing commercial authority

Accepted for implementation by the owner on 12 October 2026.

KXRA adds Decision Review (from £250), Business Improvement Sprint (from £750) and separately quoted implementation. Public prices and package preference are indicative; the immutable private proposal remains authoritative. Existing subscription prices and entitlements are unchanged.

Public requests reuse the protected enquiry inbox. Private requests reuse current custom-project RLS, explicit organisation capabilities, versioned proposals, exact acceptance, net-payment gates and delivery lifecycle. No automatic account grants or payments arise from public intake. The owner helper drafts from an authorised request; it cannot issue a contract or decide delivery acceptance. Explicit customer-organisation authority is required even for a user who operates the platform.

The initial automation is deterministic preparation and next-step guidance. Paid AI research, live one-off Stripe settlement and email delivery require their own connected acceptance. Implementation can proceed through the existing controlled payment-evidence workflow where agreed; it must not be advertised as automated card checkout.
