# ADR 0054 — One continuous industry and platform walkthrough

Status: implemented locally; production release not performed.

USER-SUPPLIED INFORMATION: the owner rejected navy/brass/ivory and separate top-navigation pages. Requested a cinematic experience like a rendered property tour, showing KXRA across industries and explaining the platform.

EXTERNAL RESEARCH: IDEO's design-services page organises its proposition around strategy, research, prototyping and implementation, with concrete business applications (https://www.ideo.com/design-services). Pentagram presents sector and discipline navigation and specific work examples (https://www.pentagram.com/). These sources inform clarity and structure, not a claim that a particular palette improves conversion. No reference identity or customer proof is copied.

DECISION / AI DESIGN JUDGMENT: pure white, graphite and restrained signal red; neutral daylight architectural footage; concise sans-serif typography. The palette is a design choice requiring owner feedback, not established market evidence.

DECISION: six connected chapters — introduction, property/hospitality, retail, professional services, KXRA OS demonstration and working approach — followed by an in-page assessment form. Primary navigation uses homepage hashes and moves the existing camera timeline. Direct links, browser Back, reduced motion and reading mode remain supported. Login and detailed/legal routes remain available separately; main marketing tabs do not navigate away.

DECISION: demonstrate Projects, Ask KXRA and Brand Studio with clearly labelled synthetic content. The sample answer is fixed explanatory copy, not a live AI response. No permission calculation, private retrieval, billing or provider call occurs in the demonstration.

FACT: a new twelve-second Seedance 2.5 request completed. The previous dark corridor asset is no longer referenced by the current homepage. Generation provenance, budget evidence and verification are in docs/architecture/consultancy-scroll-journey.md.

UNRESOLVED: owner visual acceptance; hosted release under current deployment restrictions; physical iOS/Safari behavior. Conversion performance must be measured with real prospects rather than assumed.
