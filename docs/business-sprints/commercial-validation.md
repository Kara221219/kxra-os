# Commercial validation

Category evidence: Upwork Project Catalog offers predefined fixed-price projects and consultations with scope, timelines and samples. This validates a buying format, not demand for KXRA. Sources reviewed 12 October 2026: https://www.upwork.com/services/ and https://www.upwork.com/consultations/ .

Start with one customer segment and a repeated problem that can be competently assessed. Enquiry handling and repetitive administration are hypotheses, not observed failures in a particular company. Do not pivot the entire venture portfolio based on an appealing offer.

## Twenty-one-day experiment

Create one honest demonstration. Conduct ten discovery conversations. Offer five qualified buyers a scoped pilot. Seek two paid pilots. Record objections, actual payment, delivery effort and whether the work was used. Praise without payment is not validation. Do not publish confidential case studies without separate permission.

Measure response and call rates, qualified proposals, accepted/paid proposals, delivered and accepted work, revisions, refund/complaint rate, contribution and implementation at 30 days. For each project record price, direct expenses, total delivery and sales hours, and ongoing liabilities. Use spreadsheets/code; no model financial arithmetic.

Monthly retainers require recurring useful work, such as agreed metric review and one priority improvement. Project sales are not MRR. Do not label future or one-off revenue as recurring.

## Scope pricing

Estimate research, delivery, specialist review, QA, handover and revisions. Add direct costs and an explicit contingency. Compare with customer budget and the decision's likely value without inventing benefits. Quote larger uncertainty as discovery first. No unlimited custom work is bundled into £29 platform access.

## Outreach draft structure

Use one observable reason for relevance; ask about the actual problem; offer a short conversation and labelled sample; identify KXRA and provide an opt-out. Send only under the owner's specific authorisation and applicable recipient rules. Do not mass-contact from this document.
