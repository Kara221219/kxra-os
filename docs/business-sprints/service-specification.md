# Service specification

## Offer

KXRA assesses one business problem and provides a researched recommendation, practical action plan or separately scoped implementation. Sell the useful outcome, not a page count. Do not use “bulletproof”, guaranteed ROI, fabricated credentials, invented case studies or unsupported savings claims.

Starting packages:

| Package | Starting price | Boundary | Timing after inputs |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Decision Review | £250 | One decision, up to three options, recommendation, 30-minute walkthrough | Three working days |
| Business Improvement Sprint | £750 | One process, current map, up to three options, costed 30-day plan, one working template, handover, one clarification | Seven working days |
| Implementation | Exact scoped quote | Agreed build/configuration and acceptance tests | Agreed proposal |

These are starting offers authorised for implementation, not proven willingness to pay. Exact pricing lives in an immutable database proposal. KXRA is not VAT registered; do not add VAT automatically. Recheck the owner's tax status before changing policy.

## Intake and agreement

Public intake captures package preference, name, business email, company, problem, outcome, constraints, budget band, deadline and response permission. No confidential files or credentials are requested. Submission is an assessment request, not acceptance or payment. It enters the existing protected website inbox.

Private intake uses the existing customer request model. The owner confirms fit, evidence availability, decision-maker, delivery capability and scope. An issued proposal contains deliverables, exclusions, assumptions, milestones, integer price/currency, current terms, validity, input requirements, deadline and payment gate. The customer accepts its exact hash. Starting prices and AI outputs cannot change an issued proposal.

Small bounded projects normally use payment in full. Unknown scope needs a separate discovery engagement; larger builds can use an agreed deposit/milestone structure. Work activation requires the configured net payment gate; refunds reduce net receipts. Payment records must reflect real provider/bank evidence. A return page or receipt screenshot is not authoritative settlement.

## Deliverable acceptance

The final work must address the agreed problem, distinguish evidence from assumptions, show costs and dependencies, give usable actions and explain how results will be measured. Include one working asset where agreed. Test workflows and calculations. Handover and one clarification are included only where stated. Additional scope requires an exact change agreement. Public samples must be labelled demonstrations and contain no customer confidential data.
