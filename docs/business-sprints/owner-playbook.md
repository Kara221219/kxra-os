# Owner playbook

1. Open Idea Inbox for new public enquiries. Treat identity and statements as unverified. A public enquiry does not create a customer account, project entitlement or invoice.
2. Establish the business problem and buyer: what happens now, frequency, impact, evidence, previous attempts, decision-maker, budget, deadline and who will implement.
3. Decline work beyond delivery competence. Use a separately scoped specialist review when needed. Do not sell regulated advice as a general AI plan.
4. Arrange the existing private customer invitation and membership flow after qualification. Do not auto-assign an email to a privileged account. The production owner must exist and complete MFA before administering this flow.
5. On Custom projects, open the customer's private request. Record triage. Choose a proposal starting point and Prepare sprint draft. The helper reads only an authorised request; it creates no issued proposal or payment.
6. Review and tailor draft scope, exclusions, assumptions, milestones, price, tax treatment, payment gate and current approved terms. Include required inputs and exact turnaround. Remove any statement not supported by evidence. Issue the exact proposal.
7. Customer accepts the exact proposal. Arrange payment through the agreed channel. Record real evidence with reference and currency; do not fabricate provider settlement or use PENDING as RECEIVED.
8. Activate delivery only after acceptance and the net payment gate. Verify the customer can see the assigned delivery project and no other customer's work.
9. Use the delivery template. Research, compare alternatives, build the working asset, test and review. Record decisions and work in the project, not a public asset directory.
10. Submit the agreed milestone evidence. Customer reviews the exact delivery. Use change requests for added scope, invoice/credit-note controls for commercial changes and a documented support boundary.
11. After 30 days, ask what was implemented, what changed and what remains blocked. Offer implementation or continuing review only when there is defined useful work.

Do not automatically send marketing or customer emails from this playbook. Use the existing controlled delivery integration only after connection and acceptance. Draft preparation and next-step labels reduce repetition; they do not replace owner judgment.
