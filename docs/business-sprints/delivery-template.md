# Customer delivery template

Replace brackets; delete sections outside the accepted scope. Mark drafts as drafts. Keep client evidence private.

## Engagement

Customer: [business]. Proposal: [ID, version, hash]. Problem: [agreed wording]. Outcome: [what success means]. Delivery date: [agreed]. Included clarification: [allowance].

## Evidence and current process

Describe the actual process and who performs each step. List customer-provided material and external sources with dates. Record baseline volume, time and failure rate only if measured. Identify missing information. Keep FACT, USER-SUPPLIED INFORMATION, EXTERNAL RESEARCH, ASSUMPTION, ESTIMATE, HYPOTHESIS and DECISION separate.

## Options

For each of at most three agreed options: approach; prerequisites; implementation cost; ongoing cost; staff time; risks; evidence; alternatives; reasons to reject. Use integer currency units and deterministic calculations outside LLMs. Show assumptions and sensitivity, not a single invented ROI number.

## Recommendation

Explain which option fits the budget and constraints and why. Include a “do nothing / defer” option where useful. List unresolved questions and conditions that would change the recommendation.
