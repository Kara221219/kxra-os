## Proposed consistent behavior for the current version

Preserve the accepted commitment in the new workspace policy/version rather than silently reducing it. The exact founding pack says seven calendar days of grace and read-only recovery for up to30 days **after the first failed payment**. Propose immutable first-failure lineage for each verified renewal: work grace ends at first failure+7 days, recovery ends no later than first failure+30 days, never37 days. Replayed/later failures must not restart either clock. Generation/export stop after grace, and seat growth remains subject to separately verified paid authority throughout. Own-member recovery must be read-only; final suspension follows its reviewed deadline. Scope/applicability and exact current text are not yet resolved. No shipping policy changes are made by this proposal.

Only an existing verified paid subscriber can enter this grace path. Fresh unpaid signup, incomplete initial checkout, unknown prior payment, a foreign tenant, mismatched customer/subscription, stale provider observation, revoked member/legal authority, disputed/refunded evidence or a stale binding generation cannot qualify. A current generation-checked provider renewal failure and immutable last-paid-period receipt are required; the webhook is a wakeup, not grace authority. Cancellation at paid-period end retains remaining paid access and must not be converted to immediate denial. A refund on an unrelated duplicate charge must not revoke a different verified canonical invoice; a refund of the entitlement's actual evidence charge remains a specific policy/SQL treatment to reconcile.

`tests/support/renewal-policy-review-contract.ts` is an executable test-side review model with boundary, replay, prior-payment, tenant and stale-evidence tests. It returns proposal states, explicit deadlines, `entitlement_authority:false` and `requires_policy_and_sql_integration:true`. It is outside production packages; it cannot make the zero-grace SQL grant access.
