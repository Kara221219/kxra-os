This records factual implementation constraints, not legal approval, provider activation or a promise of universal availability. The approved workspace prices remain GBP 29/79/199 monthly for 1/5/15 seats, with no VAT added under the owner's current instruction. ## Current terms delta

The current workspace terms catalogue remains empty. The reviewable workspace terms must distinguish the KXRA subscription from separately billed provider/router usage; identify customer authority over the connected account, permitted data transmission and key custody; and avoid included-credit, unlimited-use, automatic-overage or KXRA-funded fallback promises. Cancelling KXRA does not itself cancel the customer's provider account. BYOK does not mean every vendor bills the customer through the same mechanism: an OpenRouter account has its own credits, routing and fee rules.

The earlier accepted ADR 0047 renewal grace/read-only and retention promises need the separate billing/QA reconciliation. Worldwide intent and BYOK do not supersede those commitments or approve replacement terms. Current terms bytes/hash, matching legal acceptance and checkout consent remain explicit release gates.

## Engineering boundary and pending acceptance

Use separate versioned eligibility decisions for the workspace subscription, payment and each provider/model connection, with source/effective dates and review expiry. Unknown or stale evidence must produce a finite review-required state, not silent global eligibility or an unannounced UK-only product. A browser-selected country, IP address or successful payment is not conclusive business/residency/ownership evidence. Restricted subregions/persons require separate handling.

Evaluate eligibility before checkout and before key access/provider dispatch, including queued work. Preserve tenant/connection-bound encrypted keys, least access, deletion/revocation, secret-free telemetry and the explicit default-off provider gate. Do not create a KXRA-funded fallback, rotate an uninspected vault key, generate credentials or circumvent provider restrictions under this document.

The eligibility evidence contract and enforcement above remain preparation requirements, not implemented or hosted-tested capabilities. Focused final tests must cover unknown/restricted countries or parties, provider-country mismatch, unknown Gemini paid mode, stale policy and changed country/connection during queued execution, with zero key/network access after rejection. Actual provider/key-mode, customer flow and hosted acceptance remain unverified; no provider call, account change or payment was performed by this review.
