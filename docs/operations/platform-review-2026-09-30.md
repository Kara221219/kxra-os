# KXRA OS platform and commercial review

Reviewed: 30 September 2026  
Scope: actual repository, local production builds and complete hermetic acceptance contract on `codex/phase-2-completion`.

## What the platform is now

KXRA OS is a governed operating platform with two commercial paths:

1. reusable, entitlement-controlled business tools for recurring work;
2. separately discovered, scoped, accepted and priced custom projects.

For the owner, it is a portfolio and operating control plane: ideas, evidence, experiments, decisions, risks, finance, partners, files, approvals, AI capabilities, routines, run history and work logs share one permission model. For customers and partners, it is a private tenant and project workspace that reveals only current assigned scope.

## Actual strengths

- Database-enforced tenant and project isolation across 172 RLS-protected tables.
- Verified server identity and selected organization before private retrieval.
- Permission-safe Ask KXRA, private files and evidence lineage.
- Typed AI agents, skills, model policies, budgets, QA and run logs.
- Deterministic entitlement, test billing, auditable free partner access and separate custom-project controls.
- Brand Studio as the first packaged business tool.
- Twelve governed venture workspaces with project-specific hard stops.
- Independent public/private applications and bounded public enquiry ingress.
- Strong local release contract: security, browser, accessibility, recovery, CSP/SRI, secret and performance checks.

## Material gaps found

- Operating evidence existed in separate registers; there was no integrated improvement queue.
- Public messaging explained governance and visual identity more clearly than direct buyer value.
- The subscription/custom-project model was present in the private architecture but not clear enough on the public platform page.
- Public pricing intent was absent even though the owner supplied an approximately £30 monthly hypothesis.
- The platform remains private preview: live legal terms, approved price/limits, live Stripe, external AI, production storage processing, customer support policy and production release evidence are incomplete.

## Improvements completed

- Added the owner-only governed Improvement Loop and API, using current PostgreSQL evidence for repair, decision, measurement, test and demand-review signals.
- Kept automatic consequential actions at exactly zero and linked signals into existing governed workflows.
- Added negative partner API/navigation tests and desktop/mobile browser acceptance.
- Reframed the public platform around practical business value, target users and an explicit understand → prove → test → decide → improve loop.
- Added a reviewed pricing page that labels the approximately £30 monthly amount as a hypothesis, preserves separate custom-project pricing and explains free partner access.
- Updated the depth journey to present measured learning as part of the KXRA operating model.

## Commercial readiness conclusion

The platform is credible for controlled private discovery and staging demonstrations. It is not ready to take public subscription revenue or onboard unrestricted customers. Activation requires owner-approved product limits and pricing, solicitor/accountant-approved documents and policies, live Stripe and provider acceptance, production support/incident arrangements, final WAF evidence and an exact signed release manifest.
